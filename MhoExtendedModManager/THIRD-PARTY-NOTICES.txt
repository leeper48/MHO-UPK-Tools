MHO Extended Mod Manager uses the following third-party software and data.

MHModManager (mod format and behavior replicated; no code copied)
  By its original author. The mod format (manifest.json, string .json files, .mhsfx sound packs) and the library layout
  follow MHModManager 1.0.1 so mods stay interchangeable.

SharpCompress (reading .7z and .rar mod archives)
  https://github.com/adamhathcock/sharpcompress
  License: MIT
  Copyright (c) 2014 Adam Hathcock

NVorbis (decoding voice lines for playback in the Editor)
  https://github.com/NVorbis/NVorbis
  License: MIT
  Copyright (c) 2020 Andrew Ward

OggVorbisEncoder (encoding shifted voice lines as Vorbis; a managed port of libvorbis's encoder)
  https://github.com/SteveLillis/.NET-Ogg-Vorbis-Encoder
