MHO Extended Mod Manager: what's new
====================================

0.37.112
- Editor → Animations: animations whose notifies play particle effects (trails, sparks: Carnage, Daredevil, Punisher, Thor and many more) are copied with their notifies and effects. Before, such a copy left out all its notifies, including contact frames and footsteps.
- (Already in 0.37.111, not listed there:) animations that were refused because of their morph curves (Carnage's arm blades) or extra data (Daredevil, Thor) can be copied.

0.37.111
- Editor → Powers: switching powers keeps the Frame slider's place (the next power opens at the same point of its animation) instead of going back to the start.
- Editor → Powers: change single colors. "Colors in This Power" shows the colors a power uses (from its particles, materials and effect textures); click one to replace only that color with a hex color, with a Range for how close shades change too. Each part keeps its brightness. Works with the sliders, in the 3D preview and on Save.
- Click a replacement's new color for a color wheel with a Brightness slider and an Eyedropper (press and drag onto any color on screen, on any monitor). Every change updates the hex and the 3D preview at once. Under the wheel: your five most recent colors (kept on this PC) and Reset (back to the power's own color).

0.37.110
- Settings → Your Author Name: your name is filled in as the author of every new mod. New mods no longer take the author of the last mod changed in the library (often someone else's); the first time you save a new mod with an author, the app offers to remember it.
- The travel power is always the first power button (in the 3D preview and the Editor's Powers tab), the same place for every hero; the others follow by name.
- Editor → Animations: after typing in Find, the first animation in the list couldn't be selected, and a pick could be lost when the list refreshed a moment later. Both fixed; the selected animation also stays selected when Find changes.
- README and manual (Troubleshooting): Malwarebytes may flag the app as "Malware.AI…", its AI guess for new, unsigned programs. What to check and how to restore it.

0.37.109
- The Editor's SFX tab is now Audio, with Voice first and Sound Packs second.
- Editor → Powers (experimental): recolor a hero's powers. Pick a power, set Hue, Saturation and Brightness (or a preset), and watch it play with its effects in the 3D preview; Save puts the recolored power packages into the mod.
- Editor → Animations (experimental): every animation a costume plays, with its power and where it comes from. Change Animation swaps one for another hero's, team-up's or mod's; Copy From a Character swaps many at once. Only the costume's own package changes.
- New features are announced once when a new version first starts (this time: the Editor's Powers, Animations, Voice Shift and voice hint, and the grouped tabs), with a button to the part of the manual that explains them. The notice says the new Editor features are experimental and links the Discord channels for bug reports (also in the manual's Troubleshooting). Settings → What's New shows the notices again.
- Manual: step-by-step for shifting a voice; the Editor table mentions Voice Shift.

0.37.108
- Voice tab: ▶ crashed after Apply Changes rebuilt a sound file while the app was open (it read the file's old layout). Fixed; a sound that can't be read now shows a message instead.

0.37.107
- Voice tab: entries that aren't sounds (a banter target names a character, an emote an animation) have no ▶ and aren't counted as lines.
- Voice tab: when a mod's packages have no voice lines, it says where the hero's voice is in the game and offers to use it. Example: Carnage's voice comes from his Classic costume (it plays the Carnage voice package), so a mod on Carnage's base package gets the Classic costume's package with that voice, ready to shift.
- Use Another Voice lists only voices with lines (Carnage's base package, which has none, is gone; his voice is listed as Carnage).

0.37.106
- Editor → Audio → Voice: Voice Shift. Pitch, Formant and Warmth sliders (with Female → Male and Male → Female starting points); ▶ previews a line at those settings. Shift This Voice puts the shifted voice into the mod (about 30 seconds) as a sound pack the old MHModManager installs too; Remove Shift brings the original lines back. Only the costume's voice lines change: its sound effects, other costumes and other characters keep their sounds.

0.37.100
- 3D preview: Iron Man's Unibeam shows. A power that plays one animation named …_end (its start and loop belong to another power) now shows all its effects; effects only for certain characters (the Hulkbuster versions) are left out; an effect that stays on its character takes the power's offset and turn.

0.37.99
- Power effects in the 3D preview start on time for powers whose contact is at the start of their animation (contact time 0 was read as 40 %: Iron Man's Microlaser Sweep lasers came in only at the end).
- Power effects in the 3D preview, closer to the game: beams go to the socket the power aims them at (Iron Man's Microlaser Sweep sweeps level from the hands), streak-shaped sprites that don't move no longer stand up as tall bars (Microlaser Sweep, Thor's Forked Lightning), particles that live as long as their effect (a lifetime of 0) show for that long, and a beam texture's empty alpha no longer hides the beam.
- 3D preview: Bloom (Look menu, on by default): the brightest parts, the glow and the hottest highlights, get a soft halo, as in the game. It follows the Glow and Light sliders, and Create from 3D snapshots use it too (onto the model only, when the background is see-through).
- 3D preview: a Glow slider in the Look menu (how strong the glowing parts are, 0–200 %, remembered per mod), and the Light slider now goes down to 0 % (also in Create from 3D).
- 3D preview: glow comes only from a model's glow maps. A packed map without an alpha channel (DXT1) read as full glow, and a separate glow texture was added on top of it, which lit models such as Rescue all over.
- The Editor's tabs are grouped: Overview (Description, Packages), Icons (Icons, Store Images, Achievements, Other Packages), Strings, and SFX (Sound Packs, Voice).
- The 3D preview plays the same version of an animation as the game when a hero has it in more than one set (the last set listed wins: Storm's and Iron Man's reworked attacks, a costume's own idle and emotes).

0.37.90
- Moving to another hero turns the moved model's sockets (where powers attach their effects) to face the way the target hero's do, keeping their place on the model. Rescue on Iron Man fired the Unibeam 90° to the side: her chest socket faced another way than his.

0.37.89
- Rebuilding a moved costume in place (the --update-copy command) keeps everything added to the copy since: its own images, text, sound packs, pictures, tags and note. Only the moved package, the moved images and names, and the voice lines are replaced (before, the copy's own images were dropped).

0.37.88
- Moving a costume or team-up to another hero: situations the moved voice has no line for (a team-up has far fewer: no "can't do that yet", death, inventory full …) are turned off, so the target hero's own lines don't play there. The Voice tab lists them as "Not in This Voice".

0.37.87

0.37.86

0.37.85
- Apply puts a game file back to the original only when it's still what this app last wrote or last saw there. A file another program changed since (a freecam installer writing zone packages, or the same files copied in by hand) is left as it is and listed under "Left as they are", even when a mod in your list that's turned off names it. Turning a mod off still restores the files this app put in for it.

0.37.83

- Updating a moved costume keeps the copy's own changes made after the move instead of rebuilding it without them.

0.37.80

0.37.79
- Starting the app again (its icon, shortcut or the exe) brings the open window to the front, restored if it was minimized, instead of opening a second copy. A copy in another folder still runs on its own.

0.37.78
- Security: a mod whose files are named to point outside its own folder or the game folder (for example "..\..\file" or "C:\...") is refused when installed, and shows as broken in the list. Apply writes only to files directly in the game's package and language folders.
- Security: updates are signed. The app installs an update only when its signature matches the developer's key built into the app, and only from GitHub links. An unsigned release isn't offered.
- Security: archives that would unpack to more than 8 GB, or to more than 100,000 files, are refused (a "zip bomb" would fill the disk). Sound packs are limited to 1 GB of audio.
- Security: patched versions of two libraries the 3D model reader pulls in (System.Net.Http, System.Text.RegularExpressions). The manual window opens web links in your browser instead of browsing itself.
- Apply Changes checks first: the top of its window lists anything unexpected (files with no clean original, game files changed by something other than this app) and, when a file has no clean copy, suggests the Clean Game Files Folder and how to get a clean copy from Steam.
- Changed Game Files (Settings, or Review Changed Files in the Apply window): game files changed by something other than this app. Select some and Keep as a Mod (they go into one new mod in your list, turned on, so they can be turned off later) or Restore to Original (the game's own files go back, as Apply would). Files you don't act on stay as they are.
- A game file the app wrote earlier that no mod names any more (a mod that dropped a package in an edit or update, or a mod folder deleted by hand) goes back to the game's original on Apply. It used to stay changed.
- Clean game files: Settings → Clean Game Files Folder takes a clean copy of the game's CookedPCConsole (only read; its files are checked against the game's own). Previews, power effects, power icons and Apply then read the game's original packages from it instead of game files a mod has already changed.
- Backups: Settings → Check Backups lists the changed game files, whether each .bak is truly the original, and where a clean copy is, and offers to give .bak files that are the original but dated later the game's own date (only the date changes). New .bak files, and game files Apply puts back to the original, get the game's date (2024-03-14).
- Locked mods can be reordered within their locked run: drag, the arrows or Ctrl+arrows move a top-locked mod among the other top-locked mods (bottom likewise). They still can't leave the run, and other mods still can't pass the locks.
- 3D preview power buttons list every power, including each hero's travel power (Wolverine's Borrowed Ride, Black Widow's Electric Ride, Sky-Cycle, flights and sprints); a ride shows its vehicle under the hero. Powers that were missing (Captain Marvel's Photonic Devastation and others) are there now.

0.34.3
- The 3D preview is lit like the game's characters: smooth shading, sharper textures, normal-map detail, and each costume's own highlights, rim light and glowing parts.
- A Light slider under the 3D view, remembered per mod; modders can include their light level when exporting.
- The 3D view remembers the animation and the frame you left it on, per mod.
- Fixed: picking a picture and going back to 3D no longer resets the pose, zoom and animation.

0.33.2
- Nexus pages with more than one mod file (for example a default and a Variant): each of your mods follows its own file, so updating a Variant no longer installs the default. Right-click → Nexus → Choose the Nexus File picks it.
- No more flickering in the mod list when hovering, showing tooltips or scrolling.

0.33.1
- Fixed a crash when clicking the 3D tile after switching mods.

0.33.0
- The preview column shows a 3D view of the mod's character, with its animations: play, loop, reset view. The camera you set is remembered, and modders can include their preview choice and camera when exporting.
- Rounded buttons; a thumbnail strip to pick the preview picture.

0.27
- Nexus update checks use Nexus's public data only: no API key or account needed.

0.26.0
- Drag a mod card to change its priority.
- Conflicts are marked on the cards (red: partly overridden, amber: only overrides), with who overrides whom.
- The mod list's width is remembered.
- Sound packs that build on their own lines, or use nested sound containers, apply fully.

0.25.12
- Remove Mod only waits for the mod's own files to be taken out of the game, and says which.
- The status line explains itself; a click opens the Apply window.

0.25.9
- Mod cards show the hero portrait (or the costume icon, store image or inventory icon); mods without an image show the game's matching one. Pets get a Pet tag.

0.25.2
- A Browse Nexus button above the mod list.

0.25.1
- Nexus: update marks on mods with a newer version, Find My Mods (links your mods to their Nexus pages), and a Nexus bar above the mod list; sort by Updates First.

0.22.3
- Create Post: Nexus and Discord descriptions for your mod, with images, kept with the mod.
- The string editor shows what each string is used by (hero name, NPC, item, costume, power).
- Fixed: the string editor's replacement column couldn't be edited (since 0.21.1).
