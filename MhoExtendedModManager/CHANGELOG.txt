MHO Extended Mod Manager: what's new
====================================

0.60.9
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): a ground power's animations borrowed onto a high-flying one (Cyclops' ride on Spider-Man's Web-Swinging) are lowered by the height the game lifts high flyers (140), so the bike meets the ground instead of floating.

0.60.8
- 3D preview: a vehicle a power brings along (a borrowed ride's bike) plays its own animation, so it sits under its rider. It used to show in its rest pose, ahead of and below the rider.

0.60.7
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): rides borrow onto travel powers. A ride's parts live in a shared package the target hero doesn't load (Cyclops' Ride inherits them from the game's shared bike ride), so they are now copied along; the vehicle, which the game shows through a condition on the rider, is spawned for the power's length; and the rider's animations are taken from the hero's own set, not the vehicle's (Cyclops' bike has a set of its own with the same animation names). The borrowed travel power's button plays the borrowed animations in the 3D preview.

0.60.6
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): a power borrowed from another hero shows that hero's weapons. Black Widow's Snap Shot borrowing Black Panther's Triple Throw showed her own batons: the borrowed parts switched on attachments she doesn't have and showed her own hand weapons. Now each of the other hero's weapons is spawned for the power's length (as the game spawns Ant-Man's flying ants), and the hand slot it used is hidden instead of shown.

0.60.5
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): a borrowed power's button in the 3D preview plays the borrowed animation (Angela's Heavenly Charge borrowing Blade's Stake Through the Heart played her own lunge). The power's old components stay in its package, unused, and the preview no longer counts them.

0.60.4
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): the other power's hit and crit effects come along too. Their classes carry compiled script (powerfxhit's oninit, which offsets the hit effect), now copied with every reference inside the code remapped (MHO Package Modifier 2.53.30: a reader for the game's compiled script, checked on all 2,265 functions in the game's power packages; struct types too). On the census's 1,190 pairs, clean builds rose from 341 to 428.

0.60.3
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): the power list shows powers built like this one first (thrown from thrown, beam from beam, melee from melee), the others dimmed with what they are; picking one built differently gives an amber warning of what won't show. Black Panther's Triple Throw on Captain America's Shield Strike showed the daggers in hand, but nothing flew: the server decides what a power does, so a melee power never fires the other's projectiles.

0.60.2
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): a borrowed power brings the other power's own script classes along (projectiles such as Punisher's stun grenade, attachments such as Black Panther's daggers), with their defaults, components and models (MHO Package Modifier 2.53.29: compiled script classes copy, read as the game's own client reads them; classes with compiled code, such as the hit effects, are still left out). On the census's 1,190 pairs, failed borrows dropped from 171 to 90.

0.60.1
- Borrowed Look (preview feature, hidden): the borrow window offers Keep This Power's Timing only when it changes something (it redraws beams with beams, or adds start effects to a power with none), says what it changes, and suggests the whole power otherwise. It warns when enemies use the power's look too (Captain America's Boomerang Throw: the Civil War Captain America boss). Copied effects get names of their own at Save (Make Unique), so a color on a borrowed power can't reach the other hero.
- --borrow-check <target power> <donor power>: what the borrow window says for a pair (read only).

0.60.0
- Borrowed Look work resumed (still a preview feature, hidden): power components that list animation names, animation set aliases, costume classes, conditions or bones by name, and projectile / attachment classes or animation sets by reference, now copy (MHO Package Modifier 2.53.27). On the census's 1,190 hero-power pairs, borrows that fail dropped from 636 to 171.

0.50.23
- Summons keep the game's size unless the mod really changes the character's: a character's scale is read as the game does, its own else inherited from the class it extends. Jean Grey's costumes set none and inherit her base class's 0.95; a costume mod that sets 0.95 changes nothing, but 0.50.22 read the game's costume as 1 and shrank her illusions. (In the game, Jean's own illusions are 1.072, Cable's illusion size, so they're 13 % bigger than her; that stays.)
- Save also corrects summon packages the mod already ships (the ones 0.50.22 saved), and the 3D preview reads the character's scale the same way.

0.50.22
- Summons that show the mod's model (Jean Grey's illusions, Cyclops' summoned Jean) get its look right in game. Seen in game on Ultimate Jean Grey: the illusion was solid, and too big.
  • The illusion's see-through material covers material slots by number, and the game's illusion sets only slot 0 (the game's All-New X-Men model has one slot). The mod's model has 4 and uses slots 3 and 2, so none of it was see-through. Save now gives the summon its material on every slot of the mod's model.
  • The summon's size follows a real change of the character's size (corrected in 0.50.23).
  • These go into the summons' own game packages (existing files, names and GUIDs kept), added to the mod by Save: open the mod in the editor and Save, then Apply Changes.
- 3D previews: a summon's materials apply by material slot, as in game (0.50.14 put the last one on every part), and a summon is drawn at its own scale over the character's.

0.50.21
- --mesh-aliases [--all]: every character package whose class swaps models by alias, and which of them Build into Mod builds onto too (read only). Of the game's 11,844 character packages, only Ant-Man's four costumes and Howard the Duck's team-up (Iron Duck) have them.

0.50.20
- Model tab: Build into Mod also builds onto the other models the character swaps to (its MeshAliases). Ant-Man's costumes carry a second model, <costume>_small, the same skeleton at 0.4 scale, shown while he's shrunk; the build replaced only the normal one, so a user's Cassie Lang turned back into Ant-Man when shrunk. Each is fitted to its own skeleton (the small one comes out at 0.4 size).
- --mesh-sizes <package>: every skeletal mesh in a package with its height (read only).

0.50.19
- Saving a mod in the editor puts back what an earlier Make Unique renamed but today's rules keep shared, before Make Unique runs: a model shared only with the hero's own copies and summons gets its game name back (a renamed group is named back; a split one has its objects moved back beside its streamed textures). So a mod made unique before 0.50.18 (Ultimate Jean Grey: her illusions showed the game's model) is fixed by opening it in the editor and saving. Groups other characters also carry, and attachment classes, stay renamed.
- --make-unique does the same first (written to its output folder only).

0.50.18
- Make Unique keeps a hero's model shared with other heroes' summons of the same character, too (read from the game data: every model a hero's power summons). Cyclops' Call Jean carries All-New X-Men Jean's model, so Make Unique renamed it, and Jean's own illusions, which share that name, showed the game's model in game (and in the preview). Now the illusions and Cyclops' summoned Jean show the mod's model. Bosses and enemies are still renamed away (Taskmaster's boss keeps his own shield). A mod saved before: Build into Mod again (Model tab) or install it again, then Save.
- --hero-files <hero> [name part] [--unique]: the packages a hero's powers use or summon; --unique: everything Make Unique keeps shared with the hero's model (read only).

0.50.17
- 3D previews: Power FXs off turns off every power's effects, also those of a power with no animation of its own (Emma Frost's Diamond Form, Diamond Heart, Psychic Domination …), which played anyway. Its caption says the effects are off. The editor's Powers tab still shows them (they're what's being recolored).

0.50.16
- Model tab: opening it on a mod shows the mod's character right away (with several, the first by name; the mod's packages are listed by name).
- Model tab → target list: while filtering, heroes come first, then team-ups, then NPCs and the rest.

0.50.15
- Model tab → Materials: Generate Normal Map in a Color or Normal row's right-click menu (Soft, Medium, Strong, or Medium with dark parts raised): the material's normal map made from its color map, in place of the one it has. With no source picked, it replaces the normal-map texture in the mod's package.
- Every normal map the app writes (Build, Replace File, Edit in Image Editor, Generate Normal Map) is set up as the game's own are, sRGB off and normal-map compression, whatever the texture it's made from said. A normal map read as a color image shades wrong and shows seams across faces. Models built before 0.50.8 have such normal maps: build them again.

0.50.14
- 3D previews: Jean Grey's illusion is a ghost all over. The game's illusion names one material for the whole model; the preview put it on the first part only, so a costume's suit or hair stayed solid.

0.50.13
- 3D previews: summons look as in game. Jean Grey's illusions are drawn with the game's illusion glow (white and see-through facing you, pink at the edges: the material's inner and outer colours) instead of a flat blue.
- Summons for every hero (checked with --summon-census across the game's heroes: 103 powers with summons): a costume's version of a summon takes the model and animations of the summon it's made from (Squirrel Girl's squirrels, Ultron's drones); the costume pick also works where a talent sets the summon off (Nick Fury's Fireteam shows S.H.I.E.L.D. agents, not the drones of his Ultron costumes); placeholder animation sets are skipped; a summon with no animation of its own copies the hero's (Doctor Strange's images); walls, decoys and markers with no model are left out; the same summon reached twice shows once.
- --summon-census [hero …]: every hero's powers that summon a character, with the model and animations the preview uses (read only).

0.50.12
- 3D previews: summoned characters show (a user asked: Jean Grey's illusions). A power's summon stands beside the hero for the power's length, playing its own arrival or idle, with its own effects there: Jean Grey's Telepathic Illusion shows the illusion of the costume shown (the game's per-costume pick; in Phoenix form its Phoenix version), drawn with the illusion's own see-through material. When the mod carries the summon's model under the same path (Make Unique keeps illusions shared for that), the mod's model shows, as in game. Summons only a talent brings (the X-Men illusions) are left out: the preview has no talents. Full Body frames the summons too.

0.50.11
- 3D previews play smoother: up to about 60 frames a second instead of 21. The playback timer asked for 33 ms, which Windows rounds up to three of its 15.6 ms ticks (47 ms) unless another program asks for finer timing, so playback could feel slower at times without any change in the app. Timing (animation speed) is unchanged; heavy power effects still lower the drawing size while they play.
- --powers-perf <out.txt> <mod> <power> <seconds> (scratch libraries): playback speed in the Powers tab; MHO_RENDERPROF=1 logs slow frames by stage.

0.50.10
- 3D previews: transforms. Rogue's Final Form, Rocket Raccoon's Mechfall and Loki's Frost Giant show the transformed character (the costume's own version: Rogue Classic 90s, Rocket Modern …) with its own animations and props when the power that turns it on, or one of its abilities, is picked. Another power brings the hero back.
- Power buttons: a transform's abilities have buttons named after it (Swift Claws (Final Form), Missile Arm (Mechfall), Frost Orb (Frost Giant) …). Loki's Frost Giant abilities have no power class in the game files; they play the Frost Giant's animations of the same name (Frost Orb → absattack_iceorb).
- 3D previews: animated props play their own animation even when no power is found for the one playing (Rocket's mech suit). Full Body frames a transform with its props.
- Known: Rocket's mech suit shows above its pilot in the preview (about 170 units too high); its cause isn't found yet.

0.50.9
- 3D previews: Jean Grey's Phoenix form (a user's report). Picking Unleash the Phoenix, or one of its Phoenix powers, shows her as the game does in that form: her Phoenix animation set in place of the standing versions (the attacks fly), her flaming wings (and no cape), and the form's flames on her head, legs and wing trails under whatever plays. Another power takes the form off.
- Power buttons: a form's own version of a power has a button of its own, named after the form (Pyrokinetic Barrier (Unleash the Phoenix), Phoenix Charge, Flaming Debris …), playing its own effects; before, they were merged into the plain power or played its effects. Read from the game data: a talent whose condition adds an animation set, and the powers it swaps.
- Powers tab → Parts: ▶ on an effect of a power (a condition's it applies too: the Phoenix flames) shows that effect alone on the character; they said Nothing to Show.
- 3D previews: props drawn with an effect material (unlit and see-through: the Phoenix wings) show, with their own texture; they were left out.
- --hero-forms <hero>: a hero's forms, their animation sets and the powers they swap (read only).

0.50.8
- Model tab: normal maps are written as the game's are (sRGB off, UnpackMin -1, normal-map compression). Built ones were read as color images, which lit them wrong and showed seams across faces (a user's report; the old material editor's fix was the same setting). Build into Mod again to fix a model built before; color and spec maps come out as before.
- --normal-check <mod | package | folder | all>: every normal-map slot in a mod's packages, with its texture's sRGB and compression (read only).

0.50.7
- Powers tab: picking a power or a part tile in the power bar frames the whole character, as the power buttons on the Mods and Model tabs do (the Dodge tile could leave the camera on the feet). While the model loads, it frames once the animations are in.

0.50.6
- Editor: a dot (•) after the mod's name on the Edit: tab while something isn't saved yet; it goes when the change is saved, or taken back.
- Tooltips no longer cover the button they belong to: at the bottom of the screen (the Editor's Save buttons) they show above it.
- 3D views: the ground is a grid of hexagons.

0.50.5
- Editor: Save and Keep Editing no longer closes and reopens the editor. The same editor goes on with the saved mod, so the tabs, the 3D previews (model, pose, camera) and what each tab shows stay as they were, and the save is quicker.

0.50.4
- Powers tab → Parts: an effect switched off is left out of the 3D preview at once, saved or not. A recolored power's preview plays the game's files (to show the color being set), so Second Sun's head, body and limb glows still showed there after Save, though the saved packages had them off.

0.50.3
- Editor: Save and Keep Editing on the Powers tab keeps the selected power (or part tile), its Color / Parts tab and the pose in the preview (the animation at its frame, paused).

0.50.2
- 3D previews: Full Body (and Head / Bust) on a pose that isn't standing (flying, lying, a pet) frames the body where the pose puts it; a flight was framed where the character would stand, an empty floor.

0.50.1
- Faster over a long session: every editor opened and closed (each Save and Keep Editing too) stayed in memory, all of it, about 420 MB each time with the Powers and Animations tabs' 3D views, so the app got slower and slower. The shared tooltip kept the 3D previews' Look sliders, and through them the whole editor; controls now leave it when they're disposed, and those sliders are disposed with their preview. Opening and closing the editor six times now stays at about 380 MB (it went from 0.8 to 2.9 GB).

0.37.311

0.37.310

0.37.309

The automatic pose is the hero's animation frame that lies closest on the game's ghost (Taskmaster's: within 1 to 6 units), found in about a second.

0.37.307

0.37.305

0.37.304

The preview shows them at once; Save writes them into the hero's package (every costume of the hero shows them); Game's Ghosts puts the game's back. Kept with the mod.

0.37.301

0.37.300

- Powers tab: the hero's powers are icon tiles under the 3D preview (as on the Mods and Model tabs; hover one for its name; own effects, parts and added packages follow after a gap), so the preview is much bigger. - 3D preview: flipbook sprites (an explosion's or a flame's frames on one sheet) are rounded off frame by frame too, as are sprites whose material fades them with a mask (Doom's Latverian Blockade).

0.37.299
- 3D preview: square effect sprites (Vision's Solar Overcharge, and many more) are rounded off as the game draws them. A sprite whose texture doesn't fade at its border (the game's material rounds it off with its own falloff) gets a soft radial falloff: additive ones fade in color, translucent ones with no alpha of their own take their opacity from the texture's brightness. Flipbooks, streaks, beams, trails and mesh effects are left as they are. Across the game's power packages, 3,973 of 43,338 such sprites (9 %) change.

0.37.298

The preview draws glow materials with no texture (the dodge ghosts' inner and outer colors) and places effects at a ground or root socket upright.

0.37.294

0.37.293
- Model tab: a build stopped with "A generic error occurred in GDI+" when its folder's path got long (a long package name, such as a Diamond Form condition package, under Documents\My Games\…; a user's report). Windows' image library can't open paths past about 260 characters; images are now read and written through .NET, which can. Builds that worked before give byte-identical packages.

0.37.292
- Move to Another Costume: a costume whose streamed textures sit in a subgroup (Emma Frost New Tian's material_pass) moved them to a new path the game's texture cache doesn't know, and the game hung, then crashed, when it needed them (Diamond Form on Old Man Logan, a user's report). Subgroups are now split like their group: streamed textures keep their path, everything else moves. Move the original again to fix a copy made before.
- CRASH RISK now also flags a moved costume with that problem (streamed textures under a moved group's path), and says to move the original again.
- Moving a mod again onto a slot where its copy exists asks: Rebuild It (the copy is remade in place, keeping its place, on/off, tags, note and power colors), Show It or Cancel.
- What's New notice for this release (0.37.291 wasn't released: voice effects, team-up voices, the Cable / Symbiote voice fix, steeper dark glows, and this fix).
