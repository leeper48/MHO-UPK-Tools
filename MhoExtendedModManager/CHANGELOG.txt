MHO Extended Mod Manager: what's new
====================================

0.37.57
- 3D preview: props rigged to the character's own skeleton move with the character's animation, as in the game (Taskmaster's bow is in her hand and draws with the shot; it showed fixed behind her before).

0.37.56
- 3D preview: animated props (whips, nunchucks, cables, chains) play their own animation along with the character's, as in the game; they used to stand still in their resting shape at the character's feet (Taskmaster's nunchucks). One without an animation for the move being played isn't shown.

0.37.55
- 3D preview props, checked across every hero in the game: a survey of all 5,559 power and hero packages found every way the game shows, hides and swaps a character's props, and the preview now follows all of them, timed within the animation (from the power's start, its contact moment or its end; while-active windows end on time). Weapons defined in a power's own package are loaded with it (Jean Grey's, Luke Cage's and Magneto's thrown cars). Animated props (whips, chains, bikes) and props that take their mesh from a parent class (Kitty Pryde's katana) are found.
- Power buttons: a power with no icon in the game data joins the power it belongs to or is left out (43 such across the heroes; Magik's demon-form attacks), and name markup is removed (Angela). All 66 heroes' 934 buttons have a name and an icon.
- New read-only checks: --attach-census (every prop property in the game) and --props-audit (each hero's props, the powers that show them and the power buttons; lists only problems).

0.37.54
- 3D preview: weapons shown only for powers stay hidden unless the playing power switches them in (0.37.53 put Hulk's car fist on him during his roar).
- A power that shows a prop without switching slots shows it too (Hulk's Knuckledusters: the car).
- Power buttons: Ultimate Destruction's icon is found (its path leaves out the icon package); nameless internal powers without an icon (letter tiles T, T on Hulk) are left out.

0.37.53
- 3D preview props follow the power: the weapons a character can hold come from its class (a costume without its own list uses its hero's), and the power an animation belongs to switches its weapon in, as the game does (Punisher: the sawed-off shotgun for Buckshot Blast, the uzis for Submachine Guns, the sniper rifle for Sniper Shot). With no power playing, a character whose weapons are all shown on demand holds the first one listed (Punisher's pistols).

0.37.52
- 3D preview props: a costume that attaches nothing itself shows its hero's always-held props from the hero's base package (Punisher: a pistol in each hand). Props a power only shows while it plays (Punisher's shotgun, RPG ...) are left out.
- Power buttons: nameless parts of a power join the power they belong to (Punisher's grenade launchers showed as letter tiles C, F and P).

0.37.51
- Esc pauses the 3D preview's animation (in full screen: the first Esc pauses, the next one comes back).

0.37.50
- Fixed a crash when the mouse moved over the power buttons while another mod or mesh was loading.

0.37.49
- 3D preview: the framing buttons are in the order Head, Bust, Full Body.

0.37.48
- 3D preview: the Head framing button centers the head in the view (Create from 3D keeps its portrait framing).

0.37.47
- 3D preview: the playback bar (Frame slider, animation, play, Loop) is always shown, under the view. It used to vanish for good after a picture was shown.
- Faster playback: while an animation plays, the view draws fewer pixels and stretches them (fewer still while heavy power effects are on screen); a paused frame is drawn sharp again.
- Full Body, Head and Bust are icon buttons in the row under the preview, beside a bigger full screen button, all the same size. The effects toggle is called Power FXs.

0.37.46
- 3D preview: the picture strip is always under the preview; the power buttons have their own block at the bottom of the column, two rows, headed by a Powers toggle (effects on / off, moved out of Look). The Power Icons button is gone.

0.37.45
- The note box moved under the mod's details (right column); the preview gets the middle column's full height.

0.37.44
- 3D preview, more room for the view: playback (animation, play, loop, frame) in a bar over the view's bottom and Full Body / Head / Bust in its top right corner, both shown while the mouse is over the view (always in full screen); Spec, Reflect, Glow, Props, Powers and the Light / Lens sliders in a Look menu; under the view only Look, Reset View, Power Icons and full screen.
- Power buttons: the hero's powers with their icons in the strip under the preview. A click shows only that power's animations and plays the first with its effects; a second click shows them all. Power Icons switches the strip back to the pictures. In full screen they're a grid beside the view.

0.37.43
- Power effects at 10 % strength, and a power that sets off both a plain and a resource version (Thor's Ground Smash: the shockwave with and without Odinforce) shows only the plain one.

0.37.41
- 3D preview: power effects. Play an animation that belongs to one of the hero's powers and its effects play with it: particles, lightning, beams, ground decals and mesh effects, including what the power sets off (combos, procs). A mod's own copy of a power package (a recolored one) is shown as the mod has it. The Powers button turns them off (remembered).

0.37.40
- Preview: small images (costume icons) show at most 3× their size instead of stretched to fill the card.

0.37.39
- Sliders make room for their longest value: the 3D preview's frame count ("122 / 122") isn't cut off.

0.37.38
- The preview column is wider (the details column gives up a fifth of its width), and the 3D view uses the column's whole width.

0.37.37
- Fixed: a model with props but no remembered animation (most moved costumes: Unworthy Thor, Thor Infinity War on another costume) flashed and vanished in the 3D preview.

0.37.36
- 3D preview: props. The weapons the game attaches to a character (Thor's hammer, Magik's sword …) show in its hand and follow the animations; the Props button turns them off (remembered).

0.37.35
- Table cells show their tooltips in the app's dark style (they were white system boxes): a cell's own tip, or its whole text when the cell cuts it off.

0.37.34
- Fixed a crash when switching to another window ("Cannot access a disposed object: GradientGrid") after the mod details or the Editor had shown a table.

0.37.33
- Labels: "User Pick" (was "Your Pick") under the preview, "User" (was "Yours, on This PC") for a note you wrote, "User Tags" in Info, "Rename a User Tag" / "Delete a User Tag" in the tags menu, and "user" as a tag's source in tooltips.

0.37.31
- The 3D preview's full-screen button sits under the view, at its lower right.

0.37.30
- Select several mods: Shift+click (a range) or Ctrl+click (one more), then turn them all on or off (a checkbox or Space), drag them together, or move them with the priority buttons. Esc goes back to one.
- Priority keys: Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down one step, Ctrl+Home / Ctrl+End to the top or bottom (for one mod or the selected ones).
- 3D preview: Full Body, Head and Bust framing buttons, and a full-screen button (or F11) with all the controls beside the view.

0.37.29
- Move to Another Hero gives the moved model the source hero's animation tree when it uses a shared one. Doctor Strange's cape was pinned on heroes whose tree holds cape bones (Colossus, Daredevil, Punisher, Hulk, Spider-Man use one that does).

0.37.28
- Move to Another Hero always sets the source's physics weight, also when the target costume sets its own.

0.37.27
- Move to Another Hero also carries the costume's target physics weight (0 on every stock costume with a cloth cape but one), which cloth seems to need.

0.37.26
- Move to Another Hero also carries the source's physics weight over (Doctor Strange's is 1.0; Colossus's is 0), so a moved cape can be driven by its cloth simulation.

0.37.25
- Move to Another Hero now brings the model's own physics setup along (capes and coats need it to move). Costumes with their own physics setup, such as Scarlet Witch House of M, can now move too.

0.37.24
- Fixed a second crash when loading a caped costume moved to another hero (the model's link to its cape). Move such costumes again and Apply.

0.37.23
- Fixed: a caped costume moved to another hero crashed the game on load ("Bad Name Index", Doctor Strange on Colossus). Rebuild such a moved copy (move it again) and Apply.

0.37.22
- Move to Another Hero now takes costumes with capes and coats (APEX cloth) along: the cloth asset and its settings are copied with the model.
- A maximized window moved to another monitor now starts there next time (it used to come back on the first monitor until it was un-maximized once).

0.37.21
- Settings → Download Counts: how often each release of the app was downloaded from GitHub, with the change since you last looked.

0.37.20
- The window remembers its monitor, size and place: it starts where you left it (maximized if it was), for example on your second monitor. Settings → Remember Window Position turns it off.

0.37.19
- Every filter and search box has a × to clear it (the mod list, the Editor's searches, Extract, Help, the hero picker, pose search, the drop-down lists); Esc clears it too.

0.37.18
- Fixed: a few small helper bones (Black Widow, Kate Bishop, Spider-Ham) were placed wrong in some animations in the 3D preview.

0.37.17
- Fixed: some heroes' animations (for example most of Punisher's) played wrong in the 3D preview, with the legs stretched far below the floor. The game stores them in a compression the preview couldn't read yet.

0.37.16
- Fixed: a very wide drop-down (for example on a 4K screen) crashed the app when it opened.

0.37.15
- Card pictures: right-click a mod → Card Picture to choose which of its images its card in the list shows, or a custom image of your own (.PNG, .JPG or .DDS).
- Custom preview picture: right-click the preview → Custom Image.
- Mod authors: the Editor's Description tab sets the Card Picture (and the Preview Image can be a custom image too); both go along with the mod. Export offers to put your own picks into the exported mod.

0.37.14
- Editor, image tabs: Load .DDS/.PNG can change an existing replacement: select it in the replacements table (or double-click it) and load the new file. Export Original works on it too.

0.37.13
- Preview: after a mod's icons are changed (saved in the Editor or updated), the picture strip under the preview and the mod's card picture show the new images, not the old ones.

0.37.12
- Create from 3D (the icon maker): the Spec, Reflect and Glow buttons too, shared with the main 3D preview; the snapshot is taken as the view shows it.

0.37.11
- 3D preview: Spec, Reflect and Glow buttons turn the materials' highlights, reflections and glowing parts on or off (lit when on; remembered).

0.37.10
- 3D views: reflections. Materials with reflections now mirror their own environment image (the game's stock studio, sky or mountain pictures), as strong as the material sets it, stronger at glancing angles where it asks for that, tinted by the colour where it asks for that.
- 3D views: a costume's separate glow texture (full-colour emissive) now glows too.

0.37.9
- 3D views: highlights follow each material's own specular settings (strength, how sharp, the power map's range, a second highlight, the highlight colour or the diffuse-coloured highlight of newer materials), not one set of defaults.

0.37.8
- Mod header: the P / T / S badges and the Enabled pill no longer touch the Costume drop-down under them.

0.37.7
- 3D preview: a Frame slider under the animation, like the icon maker's. It follows the animation as it plays; drag it (or use the arrow keys) to scrub to a moment, and ▶ plays on from there.

0.37.6
- The mod list's top rows fit a narrow list: the Nexus strip's buttons move into its ▾ menu when there's no room, and the Priority arrows, Sort and Group are no longer cut off.

0.37.5
- Del removes the selected mod (it asks first, as Remove Mod does).
- A new look for inputs across the app: every drop-down is an accent pill like the Costume one, opening a dark list (long lists get a search box); text boxes have rounded frames; check boxes and option buttons are drawn in the app's style; buttons that open a menu (Sort, Settings, Use Another Voice …) match the drop-downs.

0.37.4
- Voice tab: Use Another Voice gives a costume a whole voice from the game: its hero's own (so its lines can be played and turned off), another costume's (Lady Deadpool, Spider-Gwen, Jane Foster …) or any hero's.
- Editor, Strings: Used By now reads all of the game's data (about 800 data files were misread before), so 212 more strings show what uses them.

0.37.2
- Editor: a Voice tab lists the costume's voice lines (what the hero says on death, level up, hits, banter, missions …). Click ▶ to hear a line; untick a line to turn it off (for example a borrowed voice naming its own team). It's saved into the mod and can be turned on again.

0.37.1
- Move to Another Hero takes the voice along: the mod's own voice lines (with its sound pack), or the original hero's voice, played by the new hero's powers.
- 3D preview: animations move bones the way the game does (rotation only, except the bones an animation set lists), so a costume on another hero no longer shows misplaced eyes or face parts.

0.37.0
- Move to Another Hero: put a costume mod's model onto a costume of a different hero (for example Savage She-Hulk on Storm's default costume). Pick the hero from their portraits, then the costume. The model, its textures, the icons and the costume's name move; the hero's own animations, powers and voice stay.
- 3D views start with a 50 mm lens (was 28.5 mm): less distortion, same framing.

0.36.9
- Create from 3D: characters start facing the camera; a Turn slider (0° = straight on) and a Reset View button. Fixed the window jittering there at larger display scaling.
- 3D preview: a Lens slider (remembered per mod), and the strip of pictures sits right under the preview.
- Moved costumes: the "(on …)" is teal for another costume of the same hero, amber for another hero.

0.36.6
- Moving a costume takes its sound pack along too (when every sound it plays is in the costume's package, as with Miles Morales). If a mod and its moved copy are both on, the pack is only added once.

0.36.4
- Move to Another Costume marks each hero's default costume ("Default", in green), in the list of costumes and on mods made for it.
- Any of the costume's text a mod changes (its name, and others such as a description) moves with it onto the new costume.

0.36.3
- A manual: Help in the top bar or F1 (search, larger or smaller text, open in your web browser).
- Remove Mod does the whole job after one question: it turns the mod off, applies the changes so the game gets its original files back, then moves the mod to the Recycle Bin.
- The Move to Another Costume window has "Turn the new mod on and the original off" ticked by default.

0.36.0
- Move to Another Costume: a mod made for one costume can be moved onto another costume of the same hero (for example Unworthy Thor, made for Age of Ultron, onto Thor Classic). The costume a mod is for shows on its card and in its header; click it (or right-click the card) and pick the costume. It's made as a new mod with the model, materials, animations, weapon, icons and costume name moved over; the original isn't changed. The "(on Classic)" at the end of a moved mod's name is shown in amber.

0.35.29
- Apply Changes closes by itself when everything went well (no Success popup); it only stays open to show what went wrong.

0.35.24
- New versions of the app are found sooner: it looks at every start and every hour while open (was once a day).

0.35.23
- 3D preview: parts whose material comes from the hero's main package (for example Savage She-Hulk's hair) show their real textures instead of plain white.

0.35.22
- Notices with a single button (Success, errors, logs) have no separate Close or OK: the colored heading is the button (Enter works too), and Success sits in the middle of its window.

0.35.21
- Create from 3D, in the Editor's icon and store tabs: make a hero portrait, costume icon or store image from a character in 3D. Frame it (turn, pan, zoom; Shift for fine zoom), pick a pose and frame, a lens and the light, show the weapon or prop the character holds, lay the game's original over the view to line things up (O toggles it), and take a snapshot at the original's exact size. Each icon remembers its own setup, and Use Previous Setup copies the last icon's pose and camera to another one.
- Click the Original, Replacement or Snapshot picture to look closer: fit, 1:1, zoom in 25% steps, full screen, and export as PNG.
- Images you load or make are compressed more carefully (sharper, less blotchy).
- Every slider works with the keyboard: click it, then the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps), Home and End.
- Nexus: downloads with Nexus's new file names are recognized again, so updating a mod clears its green Update mark.
- An Update Available button in the top bar when a new version of the app is out.
- The top bar is rearranged: New Mod, Extract, Install Mod and the game folder on the left; Update Available and Settings on the right. Menus open on the same monitor as their button.
- Clicking the Editor tab opens the mod selected in the list.
- Every popup, menu and title bar is dark with light text.
- Shorter button names; no "..." on buttons and menus.
- Safer archive handling: SharpCompress updated to 1.0.

0.34.3
- The 3D preview is lit like the game's characters: smooth shading, sharper textures, normal-map detail, and each costume's own highlights, rim light and glowing parts.
- A Light slider under the 3D view, remembered per mod; modders can include their light level when exporting.
- The 3D view remembers the animation and the frame you left it on, per mod.
- Fixed: picking a picture and going back to 3D no longer resets the pose, zoom and animation.

0.33.2
- Nexus pages with more than one mod file (for example a default and a Variant): each of your mods follows its own file, so updating a Variant no longer installs the default. Right-click → Nexus → Choose the Nexus File picks it.
- No more flickering in the mod list when hovering, showing tooltips or scrolling.

0.33.1
- Fixed a crash when clicking the 3D tile after switching mods.

0.33.0
- The preview column shows a 3D view of the mod's character, with its animations: play, loop, reset view. The camera you set is remembered, and modders can include their preview choice and camera when exporting.
- Rounded buttons; a thumbnail strip to pick the preview picture.

0.27
- Nexus update checks use Nexus's public data only: no API key or account needed.

0.26.0
- Drag a mod card to change its priority.
- Conflicts are marked on the cards (red: partly overridden, amber: only overrides), with who overrides whom.
- The mod list's width is remembered.
- Sound packs that build on their own lines, or use nested sound containers, apply fully.

0.25.12
- Remove Mod only waits for the mod's own files to be taken out of the game, and says which.
- The status line explains itself; a click opens the Apply window.

0.25.9
- Mod cards show the hero portrait (or the costume icon, store image or inventory icon); mods without an image show the game's matching one. Pets get a Pet tag.

0.25.2
- A Browse Nexus button above the mod list.

0.25.1
- Nexus: update marks on mods with a newer version, Find My Mods (links your mods to their Nexus pages), and a Nexus bar above the mod list; sort by Updates First.

0.22.3
- Create Post: Nexus and Discord descriptions for your mod, with images, kept with the mod.
- The string editor shows what each string is used by (hero name, NPC, item, costume, power).
- Fixed: the string editor's replacement column couldn't be edited (since 0.21.1).
