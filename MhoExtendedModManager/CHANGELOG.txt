MHO Extended Mod Manager: what's new
====================================

0.37.203
- Manual: a walkthrough for watching a hero's powers in the 3D preview, removing a Model target, and where power effects play; What's New notice.

0.37.202
- Model tab: MFF models whose hands and feet were exported loose (Bip001 L Hand001 / Foot001 / Toe001 hanging off the root, fingers numbered Finger042 …) get them as the Biped hand, foot, toe and fingers again, under their forearm and calf. Unmapped, their skin stayed behind and the legs and arms stretched when they moved (Kamala Khan's base model). Changes Kamala Khan (base and S01), Sandman and the Sentinels.

0.37.201
- Model tab: Biped twist bones numbered past the usual names (ForeTwist13, RUpArmTwist11, ForeTwist011 …) are mapped like their plain names (forearm twist, bicep twist, thigh twist) and turn with their limb. Unmapped, they fell back to the body, and the middle of the arm didn't follow it: Kamala S02's upper arms had a gap of no weight. Only twist bones lying along their own limb (Doc Ock's second rig is left alone). Changes Kamala S02, Doctor Strange's models, Magik, The Hood, Captain America S14, Dark Elf soldiers and Mephisto's dummy.

0.37.200
- Model tab: the hip and shoulder helpers 0.37.199 gave to the limb now go half to the limb and half to the pelvis or clavicle, as a 3ds Max helper turns halfway: all on the thigh, Kamala S02's tunic tail and buttock stretched with the swinging leg.
- Model tab: the forearm twist bone (g_*_forarm) is placed where the game's skeleton has it, about halfway between elbow and wrist. MFF rigs whose mapped twist bone sits near the wrist (Kamala S02: 86 % of the way down) had the forearm kink when the animations turn it. Applies to every MFF build.

0.37.199
- Model tab: an MFF model's helper bones at the top of a thigh or upper arm that sit on no joint (Kamala Khan S02's hip helpers at the pelvis's height, her shoulder helpers on the clavicles) now go with the thigh or upper arm, not the pelvis or clavicle: the top of the thigh no longer folds when the leg swings, and the sleeve no longer notches at the shoulder. Of the 879 MFF models only Kamala S02 has such helpers.
- Model tab: remove a target from the list (right-click it → Remove, or Delete). A package the tab added from the game leaves the mod with its build; one of the mod's own packages can go back to the mod's copy (taking the model off) or leave the mod. Cancel in the editor brings it all back.

0.37.198
- Model tab: a model built onto a team-up or NPC whose skeleton has helper bones beside the limbs (g_l_uprarm, g_l_uprleg, g_l_lwrlegmid …, turned by its animations) gets the share of the limb the stock mesh gives them (from its nearest vertices), so the upper arms and thighs follow as the stock model's do. Player builds don't change.

0.37.197
- 3D preview: a power's "_loop" animation also plays what started with the power and keeps going through it (a looping effect from the power's start): Cyclops's Visor Off beam showed nothing in its loop. Applies to every power with start / loop / end animations.

0.37.196
- 3D preview: a missile's explosion plays where its flight reaches the target, when it gets there (Kate Bishop's Nerve Gas Arrow burst on her own chest), and what a missile, hotspot or summon sets off goes off there too (Shrieking Arrow's explosion hits).
- 3D preview: an effect whose particles already start where its socket is (their own start offset as long as the socket's) is placed from the socket's bone, turned as the socket: Ms. Marvel's Disengaging Shot fired from above her head.

0.37.195
- 3D preview: beams whose material has no texture (a plain solid beam: Ms. Marvel's Photonic Devastation) show as a soft band in their own color; they were left out.

0.37.194
- 3D preview, power effects, for every hero (checked over all 66 heroes' 1,760 powers): what lands on the ones a power hits now plays at the target point in front of the hero instead of on the hero: a power's hit effects (timed by the hit or at socket_hit), and conditions from hostile blueprints (stun, damage over time, slow, bleed, knockup, freeze, vulnerability …), debuffs and hit-like looks. Buffs, shields, heals and auras stay on the hero.
- 3D preview: beams aimed at a standard socket (socket_hit, socket_head …) end at the target, at that height (Vision's Tri-Beam ran into his own chest). A caster's own aiming helper (Iron Man's laser targets) is still used.
- 3D preview: other powers a power links to (talents, combos) that have animations of their own aren't played with it (Solar Bolt showed Ground Smash's and Death from Below's dives and rings).
- 3D views: P (play / pause) also puts the keyboard on the frame slider, so the arrow keys step frame by frame (Shift: 5).

0.37.193
- 3D preview: a beam that keeps itself up (no spawn rate or bursts: Vision's Channeled Solar Beam) shows now, from its socket (his eyes) to the target.
- 3D preview: what a power applies through a hotspot or summon it makes plays on the one it hits, at the target point in front of the hero, not on the hero (the beam's impact and vulnerability glow had piled up on Vision's chest). Conditions the power puts on the hero himself (Healing Nanites) stay on him.

0.37.191

Every panel size and fold (the mod list, preview, the Model tab's panels and log, the Editor's Tags and Note) is remembered from one start to the next, like the window's place.
- Fixed (0.37.188): the app ran without its own screen-scaling support (blurry, and some tooltips stretched); it's sharp again at 125 %, 150 %, 175 %.

0.37.189

0.37.188

- Model tab: drag the dividers between Source, Preview and Target, between Target's list and its tabs, and above the log; click a heading (Source, Target, Material, Size in Game, Log) to fold that panel. Sizes and folds are remembered. The log starts smaller.
- Editor: the ▴ next to Version folds the Tags and Note rows away (more room for the tabs; remembered).
- Model tab: the first log line says plainly what's there: "Source: 1,448 models in your MFF folder. Target: the mod's own packages, or From the Game: ...".

0.37.186

0.37.185

0.37.184

0.37.183

0.37.182

A change goes right into the mod's copy of the package, encoded like the original (DXT1 / DXT5, its size, with mipmaps); the preview shows the mod's package as it is now.

0.37.181
- New: a busy ring. One in the top bar turns while anything runs in the background (3D models, animations and effects loading, the Model tab's preview, builds, exports, Blender), one beside the Model tab's status for its own work; point at it to see what's running.
- Model tab → Materials: Edit in Image Editor opens the selected map in your image editor (GIMP, Photoshop, Corel PHOTO-PAINT, Affinity Photo, Krita and Paint.NET are found; Settings → Model → Choose Image Editor picks one or any other program). Every save there comes back into the tab as that map, like Use a File; Back to Automatic or Undo takes it back.

0.37.180
- Model tab: Single Model reads more kinds of model: OBJ, DAE (Collada) and STL directly, and Blender files (.blend) and XNALara / XPS models (.xps, .mesh, .mesh.ascii) through Blender, which makes an FBX of them once (until the file changes). XPS models come with their skeleton, weights and textures; an MFF character saved as XPS (Bip001 bones) is read as an MFF character. Browse for an FBX is now Browse for a Model.

0.37.179
- New: Size in Game (Model tab). Make a character bigger or smaller in game (5-400 % of the game's size). It's written as the mesh's scale, as the game sizes its own costumes, so the animations keep their shape and the feet stay on the ground. It works without a model too: with no source picked, Build into Mod writes only the size. Match Steps to Size (on by default) plays the run and walk slower for a bigger character and faster for a smaller one, so the feet don't slide.
- New: models on NPCs, enemies and team-ups. Target → From the Game lists the game's NPCs and Enemies and Bosses that share the heroes' skeleton (672), and the team-ups. A model built onto one shows wherever the game uses that character.
- Model tab: team-ups and NPCs whose animations set every bone's position (the heroes' only turn the bones) pulled a new model's arms and shoulders back to the old model's shape. The build now makes them work like a hero's, and the preview shows them that way.
- New: Own Effects in the Powers tab. A mod holding an NPC, enemy or team-up package lists its own effects (glows, trails: the Sinister clones' red glow) and its own powers (read from the game data), recolored and played in the 3D view like a hero's power. Add It to This Mod brings in the package a character is built on (the Sinister Medal pets use the mob clones').
- New: Opacity in the Powers tab fades an effect out; at 0 % it's switched off, and a hologram team-up (the Danger Room Wolverine) shows its own materials, in the preview and in game.
- Model tab: Single Model has one Browse for an FBX. An MFF character's model is recognized and read with its folder, an FBX with a skeleton the tab doesn't know is rigged for you, and textures are found in its folder, its subfolders and the folders next to it (such as Textures).
- Model tab: the source list says MFF Repository (your MFF rip) or Single Model (one file).
- Blender: Texture Paint comes back too. Ctrl+S saves the painted images, and each comes into the Materials tab as that material's map (in a scene opened before this version, save the image with Image → Save).
- Blender: bones moved in Edit Mode come back with Ctrl+S, from any Open in Blender scene.
- Model tab: leaving the tab or Save Changes with a change not built yet asks: Build Now / Build and Save, or carry on without it.
- Model tab: ⛶ in the log shows it full screen (Esc comes back).
- Create from 3D: Save Large PNG saves a big copy of the snapshot (up to 2048 px) for a promo picture.
- Fixed: Import FBX crashed for FBX sources.
- Manual: Walkthroughs with screenshots for the common jobs (Help, or F1).

0.37.157
- New: the Editor's Model tab (experimental). Put a model of your own on one of the mod's costumes: a Marvel Future Fight character from your own MFF rip, or an FBX file. It's fitted to the hero's skeleton, so the hero's animations, powers and props play on it. Build into Mod puts it in the costume's package; Apply Changes puts it in the game.
- Model tab: FBX files rigged with MHO bones, Mixamo bones or other skeletons (matched by name or guessed from the skeleton's shape), or not rigged at all: a plain mesh is stood up, scaled to the hero and rigged in Blender with automatic weights, fingers included; open the rig in Blender to fix weights, each Ctrl+S reloads it.
- Model tab: Full Export writes the model and all the hero's animations as FBX, or opens them in Blender; every Ctrl+S there sends your changes (mesh, weights, animations) back to the tab.
- Model tab → Materials: each map and where it comes from (color, normal, spec, the game's own packed spec maps in any of its six layouts, spec color, glow, alpha), with your own files, OpenGL normal maps, spec maps made from the color map (five recipes), Tag Colors (say what each color is: metal, skin, leather, cloth or glow; an eyedropper for small details and a reach slider), glow maps, a view of any map or spec channel on the model or large, and Export Maps.
- Model tab: Parts (with smoothing for low-poly models), moving capes and long hair for heroes without them, the Bone Map (pick bones, smooth weights, a weight-paint view), and Undo / Redo for all of it. Settings → Model: the MFF folder, Blender, the optional add-on, and Clean Up Exports and Rigs.
- Buttons are icons now, almost everywhere (tabs keep their words, and so do Apply Changes, Update Available, Sort, Group and the answers in message windows). Point at one for its name, in bold, and what it does.
- Tooltips: they show for grayed-out buttons too, and in pop-up windows (they didn't before). They open by the mouse; long ones could open at the far left of the screen.
- 3D views: the animation list is half as wide, and Look (an eye), Reset View, the framing buttons and full screen sit on its row; the row under the caption is gone, so the view is taller.
- 3D views: P plays and pauses the animation (not while typing), in the Mods tab, the Editor's Powers and Animations tabs and full screen; Esc now pauses the Animations tab's preview too.
- One Extract: the top bar's Extract button is gone (the Extract tab stays).
- The Nexus strip's Check for Updates, Find My Mods and Browse Nexus are icons; on a narrow list they still fold into its ▾ menu, by name.
- The optional MHO Actions Blender add-on shipped with the app is 1.55.2 (Settings → Model installs it).

0.37.112
- Powers for costumes named differently from their hero: the hero now comes from the game's costume data, so Kate Bishop (a Hawkeye costume) lists Hawkeye's powers in the Powers tab and the 3D preview, and her mod can recolor them (the power packages go into the mod on Save).
- 3D preview: vehicles and props face the right way. A prop's own turn (its attachment's OffsetRotation: Hawkeye's and Kate Bishop's Sky-Cycle, Ghost Rider's bikes, Doom's throne, Lockheed, Rocket's rides, Venom's arms) and a model's built-in turn (its RotOrigin: Blade's motorcycle, Ghost Rider's chains) are applied; both were left out, so those riders sat 90° across their vehicles.
- 3D preview: poses held for a whole animation are no longer mirrored on some bones (hands, thumbs, capes, a leaning torso): Cyclops leans into his handlebars on the bike, Taskmaster holds her shield flat in idle.
- 3D preview: a ride's vehicle always gets its ride animation (it could load before the character's props and show turned 90° or not at all: Blade's bike).
- 3D preview: riders sit on their bikes. A prop with its own animation (Cyclops's, Wolverine's and Blade's bikes) now goes where its animation puts it; it was kept in place like the hero, which left Cyclops floating behind and above the seat.
- 3D preview: slash afterimages follow the power's color change (black hides them).
- 3D preview (and the Powers tab): powers that bring in other characters show them. Animated actors (305 in the game: Captain America's Avengers Assemble, the Fantastic Four, Beast leaping in for Cyclops, Magneto's Sentinels, Black Panther's panthers…) appear at their moment and play their own animation, by the hero or at the target; slash afterimages (Angela, Taskmaster, Black Cat, Nightcrawler) are glowing see-through copies that fade out. Actors of a later moment (a dismount) and of other costumes are left out. Summoned pets, walls and turrets come later.
- Editor → Animations: animations whose notifies play particle effects (trails, sparks: Carnage, Daredevil, Punisher, Thor and many more) are copied with their notifies and effects. Before, such a copy left out all its notifies, including contact frames and footsteps.
- (Already in 0.37.111, not listed there:) animations that were refused because of their morph curves (Carnage's arm blades) or extra data (Daredevil, Thor) can be copied.
- What's New: a notice for the Powers tab's single colors, color wheel and eyedropper (Settings → What's New shows it again, and Older the earlier one).

0.37.111
- Editor → Powers: switching powers keeps the Frame slider's place (the next power opens at the same point of its animation) instead of going back to the start.
- Editor → Powers: change single colors. "Colors in This Power" shows the colors a power uses (from its particles, materials and effect textures); click one to replace only that color with a hex color, with a Range for how close shades change too. 0.37.78
- Security: a mod whose files are named to point outside its own folder or the game folder (for example "..\..\file" or "C:\...") is refused when installed, and shows as broken in the list. Apply writes only to files directly in the game's package and language folders.
- Security: updates are signed. The app installs an update only when its signature matches the developer's key built into the app, and only from GitHub links. An unsigned release isn't offered.
- Security: archives that would unpack to more than 8 GB, or to more than 100,000 files, are refused (a "zip bomb" would fill the disk). Sound packs are limited to 1 GB of audio.
- Security: patched versions of two libraries the 3D model reader pulls in (System.Net.Http, System.Text.RegularExpressions). The manual window opens web links in your browser instead of browsing itself.
- Apply Changes checks first: the top of its window lists anything unexpected (files with no clean original, game files changed by something other than this app) and, when a file has no clean copy, suggests the Clean Game Files Folder and how to get a clean copy from Steam.
- Changed Game Files (Settings, or Review Changed Files in the Apply window): game files changed by something other than this app. Select some and Keep as a Mod (they go into one new mod in your list, turned on, so they can be turned off later) or Restore to Original (the game's own files go back, as Apply would). Files you don't act on stay as they are.
- A game file the app wrote earlier that no mod names any more (a mod that dropped a package in an edit or update, or a mod folder deleted by hand) goes back to the game's original on Apply. It used to stay changed.
- Clean game files: Settings → Clean Game Files Folder takes a clean copy of the game's CookedPCConsole (only read; its files are checked against the game's own). Previews, power effects, power icons and Apply then read the game's original packages from it instead of game files a mod has already changed.
- Backups: Settings → Check Backups lists the changed game files, whether each .bak is truly the original, and where a clean copy is, and offers to give .bak files that are the original but dated later the game's own date (only the date changes). New .bak files, and game files Apply puts back to the original, get the game's date (2024-03-14).
- Locked mods can be reordered within their locked run: drag, the arrows or Ctrl+arrows move a top-locked mod among the other top-locked mods (bottom likewise). They still can't leave the run, and other mods still can't pass the locks.
- 3D preview power buttons list every power, including each hero's travel power (Wolverine's Borrowed Ride, Black Widow's Electric Ride, Sky-Cycle, flights and sprints); a ride shows its vehicle under the hero. Powers that were missing (Captain Marvel's Photonic Devastation and others) are there now.
